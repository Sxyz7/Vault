# 🎯 Current Goal

The current focus is building the core collecting experience, including:

- Inventory management
- Digital collectibles
- Item rarity system
- Collection tracking

These systems form the foundation for future features such as trading and the marketplace.

---

# 🚀 Future Vision

Vault aims to become a modern community platform where players can:

- 💎 Collect unique digital items
- 📦 Open crates
- 🤝 Trade securely with others
- 🏆 Earn badges and titles
- 🧸 Unlock companions
- 🎨 Customize their profiles
- 📈 Build valuable collections
- 🌍 Participate in community events
