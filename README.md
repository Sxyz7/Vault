## 📖 About

Vault is a community-driven digital collectibles platform where players can discover, collect, trade, and showcase unique virtual items.

Whether you're opening crates, building rare collections, earning exclusive badges, or trading with other collectors, Vault is designed to bring the excitement of collecting into one secure and interactive marketplace.

---

## ✨ Planned Features

- 📦 Collect unique digital items
- 🎁 Open crates to earn rewards
- 💎 Build rare collections
- 🤝 Secure player trading
- 🏆 Earn exclusive badges
- 👤 User profiles
- 📊 Collection tracking
- 🌍 Community marketplace
